# First Shift

[TryHackMe Module - First Shift](https://tryhackme.com/module/first-shift)

Step into the chaos at ProbablyFine MSSP and prepare to handle a variety of challenges during your shift, from basic tasks to complex incidents. The alerts are piling up, and your team is counting on you. Are you ready for the First Shift?

Revisit the First Shift CTF from winter 2026 through six hands-on challenges preparing you for the SOC role, starting from easy CTI, EDR, and phishing analysis challenges, and continuing to much harder network analysis and Splunk scenarios that will truly test your SOC skills and put you into a day in the life of a real analyst.
